Returns & Refunds

Your satisfaction matters to us. If something isn’t right, we’re here to help.

1

Eligibility for refunds

  • The item must be returned within 14 days of the delivery date.
  • It must be in the original packaging with all tags attached.
  • It must be unused, unwashed, and in the same condition you received it.
2

Non-refundable items

  • Gift cards.
  • Hygiene products (e.g., socks, underwear).
  • Defective or incorrect items are always eligible for return or exchange.
3

Refund process

  • Contact our customer service within 14 days of receiving your order.
  • Provide your order number and the reason for the return.
  • Once your return is received and inspected, we notify you whether your refund is approved or rejected.
  • Approved refunds go back through the original payment method.
4

Return shipping

  • Customers cover return shipping costs unless the item is defective or incorrect.
  • We recommend a trackable shipping service so we can confirm we received your return.
